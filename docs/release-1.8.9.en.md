## OS Studio 1.8.9 — GSX Termine → Kalender 1.2.0

- **Rescheduled appointments** (“Neu festgelegt”) are now skipped like cancelled ones.
- **Clean Downloads:** The exported calendar file is deleted again 2 minutes after it opens in Apple Calendar.
- To apply, click **Update Folder** under Extras › Chrome and click ↻ on the extension in `chrome://extensions`.

Universal for Intel and Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later. Build 59.
