## OS Studio 1.8.10 — GSX Termine → Kalender 1.2.1

- **One hour per appointment:** Appointments are always added to the calendar for one hour, e.g. 3:00–4:00 PM (previously 20 minutes).
- To apply, click **Update Folder** under Extras › Chrome and click ↻ on the extension in `chrome://extensions`.

Universal for Intel and Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later. Build 60.
