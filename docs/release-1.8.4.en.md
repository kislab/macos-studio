## OS Studio 1.8.4 — a clearer installer list

- **One row per version:** Builds for specific Macs can be expanded below the version. The build for your Mac comes first.
- **Which Macs:** Builds for specific Macs show their models, e.g. “For: 4 × MacBook Pro”. Hover for the full list.
- **“Only for this Mac” switch** right above the list.
- **Update window:** Now shows what has changed.

Universal for Intel and Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later. Build 54.
